## Community post

We are turning the RFC series into small, useful learning tools. This edition explores RFC 2, a legacy Host Software proposal, through Handshake Lab. Step through success, simultaneous requests, timeout and unexpected-response scenarios. A teaching trace, not a complete NCP implementation. Link 7 and timeout values are examples; the host range is a teaching constraint. RFC 2 is an incomplete archive with its first page reported missing.

Try it: https://sreenivas-sadhu-prabhakara.github.io/rfc-0002-handshake-lab/
Source and contributions: https://github.com/Sreenivas-Sadhu-Prabhakara/rfc-0002-handshake-lab
Original document: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2.txt

## Teaching exercise

Choose the collision scenario and step through it. Swap host IDs and repeat. Then select a missing reply. Explain why silence cannot count as a successful connection.
